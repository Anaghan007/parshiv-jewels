Diamond & Jewellery Guide

Buying fine jewellery is an emotional and financial decision. Start by defining the occasion — daily wear demands sturdy settings and rhodium-coated 925 silver, while bridal pieces can carry heavier stones and intricate jali work. Always check the metal stamp (925 for sterling), ask for stone certificates, and compare making charges separately from metal weight. At Parshiv, every bill itemises silver weight, stone cost and making charges so you always know what you pay for.

  • ◆Buy jewellery after measuring size — use our Size Guide below.
  • ◆Prefer hallmarked metal and certified stones, always.
  • ◆Keep the bill & certificate together in one pouch forever.

Diamond Guide — the 4Cs

Cut

The only C shaped by man. A well-cut stone sparkles even in candlelight. Prioritise Cut above all.

Color

Graded D (colourless) to Z. For silver settings, D–H range looks perfectly white.

Clarity

VS–SI stones are eye-clean and save 20–30% over flawless grades.

Carat

Weight, not size. A 0.90ct stone looks identical to 1.00ct but costs far less.

Jewellery Care Guide

Do

  • ◆ Store each piece in an anti-tarnish pouch
  • ◆ Wipe with the provided microfibre cloth
  • ◆ Put jewellery on after perfume & makeup
  • ◆ Clean monthly in lukewarm soapy water

Don't

  • ◆ No hot springs / chlorinated pools
  • ◆ No direct contact with sprays & acids
  • ◆ Don't sleep in prong-set stones
  • ◆ Never rub oxidised finish with silver dip

Certification Guide

  • ◆BIS Hallmark (925) — Indian Govt.-licensed assay stamp proving 92.5% pure silver.
  • ◆Maker's Mark — our atelier's initials laser-marked beside the hallmark.
  • ◆Stone Certificate — graded report for diamonds & gemstones (IGI/GIL partner labs).
  • ◆Serial & QR — scan the tag to view your piece's manufacturing record.
